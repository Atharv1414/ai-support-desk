# Delivery times

## Standard delivery

Standard delivery takes three to five working days and is free on orders over £40. Below £40 it costs £3.95.

## Next day delivery

Order before 8pm for next day delivery, Monday to Saturday. It costs £6.95. Large items such as ovens and fridges cannot go next day.

## If it is late

If your order has not come two working days after the date we gave, we treat it as late. We open a search with the courier, which takes up to three working days. If it is not found we send another or refund you, whichever you prefer.
