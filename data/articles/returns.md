# Returns

## How long you have

You have 30 days from delivery to return anything, for any reason. You do not need the original box, but the item must be protected in the post.

## How to send it back

Print a free label from your account, or show the code at a parcel shop. We collect large items such as ovens and fridges free of charge.

## Opened items

Opened items can be returned if they are clean. Personal care items cannot be returned once opened unless they are faulty.
