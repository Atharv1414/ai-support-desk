# Faulty items

## First 30 days

If something stops working in the first 30 days, you can have a refund or a replacement.

## After 30 days

After 30 days and within the guarantee we repair it, or replace it if it cannot be repaired. Repairs take up to ten working days.

## Error codes

E1 means the water tank is empty. E4 means the heater has cut out: unplug it, leave it 30 minutes and try again. If E4 comes back, book a repair.
