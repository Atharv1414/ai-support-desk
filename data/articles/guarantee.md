# The two year guarantee

## What is covered

Every appliance has a two year guarantee from the day it was delivered. It covers parts and labour for faults in normal home use.

## What is not covered

Accidental damage, wear to filters and seals, limescale damage and commercial use are not covered.

## Registering

You do not have to register. Keep your order number or receipt as proof of purchase. The guarantee passes to a new owner with the receipt.
