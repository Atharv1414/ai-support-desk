# When refunds arrive

## Timing

We refund within two working days of your return reaching our warehouse. Banks then take three to five working days to show it.

## Expired cards

If your card has expired, the bank passes the refund to your new card. If the account is closed, tell us and we will send a bank transfer.

## Part refunds

A refund is smaller than you paid if a part was missing from the return or the item was used beyond testing.
